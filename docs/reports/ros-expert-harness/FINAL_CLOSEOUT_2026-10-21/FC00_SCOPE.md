# Final closeout scope

This is final acceptance and archival of bounded ROS engineering in simulation,
not a new RSK architecture or a general autonomous robotics claim.

1. Audit current source and preserve historical evidence (FC-00).
2. Normally merge the thin report/protocol adapter before registering cases.
3. Freeze a separate seven-scenario/two-environment-seed contract (FC-01).
   Missing original cells stop entry. Planned cells are recorded as UNAVAILABLE,
   never as fabricated hashes or successful episodes. REPLAY and SYNTHETIC
   evidence cannot silently pass the original-input gate.
4. Only an admitted original P1 and an actual host-accepted model proposal can
   reach separately admitted existing SDK/rosclawd validation (FC-02).
5. Audit all outcomes, including NOT_RUN_NO_GO, and normally merge final
   documentation and evidence indexes (FC-03/04).

Four decisions/300 seconds per model episode and a maximum of 14 episodes are
upper bounds, not an obligation to spend requests when originals are missing.
The two environment seeds do not establish model sampler seed control. The
physical maximum is six independently owned Worlds (two Before/After/Rollback
triples), with two canonical goals in each After. This protocol creates no
physical evaluator or execution entry.

The old RskPilotProtocolV2 retains its five strata, one seed and original gates.
Old results, safety limits, source/body/run checks, leases, permits, watchdog,
controllers, verifier and MCP-to-rosclawd boundary remain unchanged. A model
report is not proof of correct abstention; host refusal is not model correctness.

`v1_scoped_done` remains the prior limited SIM/negative-research conclusion.
Final work-package completion, model decision quality, current physical outcome
and general autonomy are independently reported. Missing entry evidence or a
negative model result can close this work package honestly; neither can support
a claim of a new successful model-to-motion demonstration.
